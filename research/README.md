# Research library — no production assets

This directory contains initial public research **leads** and rights-gated media candidates, not final site content. Seeded 2026-11-06 from official provincial publications, PIA, Embassy of Japan, news and third-party social indexes.

Read [research/media source register](../docs/04-research-media-source-register.md), then execute [Antigravity Opus Low mission](../docs/05-antigravity-opus-low-research-mission.md) on a separate branch. The mission must EXPAND and verify these records rather than overwrite or imply they are already publication-approved.

**No image files are approved.** Original media URL fields can be blank until original owners/individual photos are identified. Do not download and publish Facebook photos, paid Getty images, editorial imagery or municipal seal without applicable permission. A source is only confirmed as a source if its URL was visited; claims may still require additional verification.

All `research-local/` caches and temporary Facebook screenshots stay off public GitHub. Use documented rights-approved assets under `public/media/approved/` only after source and brand approval.
