## Executive narrative — why Valencia deserves this website
Valencia's own name is a story. Before it was a town it was **Panangatan**, the riverbank where Dimiao and Lila fishermen *sang-at* — lifted — their boats to safety from the southwest monsoon (PPDO 2025; Wikipedia). That single image of people lifting something precious above the storm ties together everything a visitor or decision-maker should take away:

- **Shelter and resilience** → the Sang-at Festival, created in 2022/2023 and already dancing at Sandugo 2026.
- **Craft that endures** → the 1882 two-tone hardwood floor of the Sr. Sto. Niño Parish Church, a declared Important Cultural Property.
- **Land that rises from the sea** → Badiang's cold spring falling into the Bohol Sea; ridgelines at Banderahan and Ugjong; caves and Kinawa's "cauldron" falls.
- **Standing up together** → *BARUG* means "to stand". The governance agenda (BARUG VALENCIA) fits the place's story without any need for invented slogans.

The site should **show** this first and offer services second: a cinematic opener (river → boats → festival), then Explore, Culture, People, Progress, and a compact "Serbisyo/Services" strip that stays visible.
