# M00 — Valencia, Bohol: Discovery, source register, and publication governance
Status: **working discovery baseline**, 2026-11-06. Owner: Kirch-Nairu. No municipal endorsement, official designation, branding authority, domain, or release approval has been established by this repository.

## 1. The central product decision
**Approved working creative direction for exploration:** **BEHOLD VALENCIA** — a photography-led, destination-promotion and cultural-identity website for Valencia, Bohol, with sourced municipal progress and reliable government utilities as supporting functions. A beautiful, locally authentic executive presentation is a core outcome; public services must remain easy to find but must NOT visually dominate the homepage. See [creative brief](./03-behold-valencia-creative-brief.md) and [tourism/media research](./04-research-media-source-register.md).

Two possible identities must remain distinct until authorization is documented:
- **Official LGU website:** requires written designation/approval, authorized logo/seal, approved offices, current service charter, municipal publication owner, official domain policy, and applicable government requirements.
- **Independent Valencia information guide:** may publish verifiable public facts, resident-oriented guides and licensed images, but MUST NOT call itself an official municipal government channel, imitate government authority, or present unofficial documents as operative service requirements.

No logo, elected-official roster, hotline, payment instruction, permit checklist, or pricing is approved by discovery alone.

## 2. Verified research findings (do not confuse municipalities)
- Municipality: Valencia, **Bohol**, Philippine Standard Geographic Code **0701247000**; PSA reports **28,535 population (2024 POPCEN)** and **35 barangays**. PSA is the source of truth for current census/PSGC, not search snippets for Valencia in Negros Oriental or Bukidnon: https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/0701247000
- Provincial Planning and Development Office maintains a Valencia municipal map series (basic, hazard, statistical, thematic); maps are reference products and should retain publication/source and version context: https://ppdo.bohol.gov.ph/maps/municipal-maps/
- Official Provincial Tourism Office lists **Sto. Niño Parish Church, Valencia** among Bohol cultural sites, noting twin bell towers, retablos and wooden parquet floors. This is a supported heritage editorial candidate, not proof that third-party images are licensed: https://tourism.bohol.gov.ph/bohol-island-unesco-global-geopark/
- A separately hosted **2015 Valencia Citizen Charter** exists; it is historical research ONLY and MUST NOT be used as current public instructions, fees, office names or contact data: https://www.yumpu.com/en/document/view/51017742/valencia-citizen-charter-municipality-of-valencia-bohol
- RA 11032 specifies the items required in up-to-date government service standards and public-facing Citizen's Charter, including steps, required documents, responsible officers, time, fees and complaints procedure. Obtain the municipality's CURRENT approved edition before publication: https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96391
- DILG's Full Disclosure Policy requires LGUs to disclose designated financial documents, including through its official FDP Portal. The municipal website can offer a clear, accurate transparency index and authoritative links, but cannot falsely assert compliance: https://ncr.dilg.gov.ph/programs/
- Google Antigravity officially documents editor/terminal/browser agent activity and artifacts. This supports an independently evidenced frontend evaluation; it does not prove any test has yet run: https://antigravity.google/docs/ide/overview

## 3. Source-of-truth priorities
1. Current municipal **signed/authorized** records and the authorized municipal public-information officer.
2. PSA / PSGC for census and barangay counts.
3. Bohol PPDO for official map references; Bohol Provincial Tourism Office and National Museum sources for heritage.
4. DILG, ARTA and government gazettes for applicable standards.
5. Documented local interviews and directly captured, permission-cleared field photography.
6. Third-party news/travel pages as *leads* to verify, NOT production truth.
7. Generated model text and unsourced social posts have zero independent evidentiary weight.

For each claim record: `claim_id, exact_claim, source_url/file, publisher, date_published, date_checked, geography, status (verified/needs-LGU-review/stale/rejected), reviewer, last_reviewed_at`.

## 4. Discovery collection checklist
**Municipal governance:** official status and written authorization, approved seal and colors, office hierarchy and office directory, current administration and terms, signed current Citizen's Charter, public office hours, valid phones/emails, official emergency response contact, privacy officer/publication owner, applicable document archives, source for notices.

**Municipal profile:** exact barangay names and administrative codes, settlement history, annual festivals, local livelihood, market economy, agriculture/fisheries, place names and corrected local pronunciations. Historical dates require provenance and cross-checking.

**Services:** service owner, plain-language service name, beneficiary, full requirements, fees, steps, estimated processing time, office/window, hours, forms, valid dates and complaint route. Until endorsed: label as pending verification or omit from public production.

**Visitor information:** permitted destination names, how to get there, accessibility/safety, managed hours and fees (when verified), responsible contact and dated photography. No copied Google Maps photos, screenshots, tourism descriptions or stock images without rights.

**Public documents:** signed/issued date, effective date, document class, issuing office, official source/URL, PDF accessibility, supersession and retention/version history. Avoid releasing internal documents or personal data.

## 5. Photography and media shot list (to commission / request rights for)
Priority A: high-resolution dramatic location-verified Valencia coastal/aerial/landscape hero; 2026 Sang-at dance, people and portraits with rights; Sto. Niño church and authentic community/cultural imagery. Secondary: municipal town center/market, approved progress/project photography and service facilities.
Priority B: barangay life, farms, fishing and coastal livelihoods, community gatherings/approved festivals, local crafts/food, municipal facilities, historically important architecture. Potential spring sites mentioned in travel media need local confirmation before labeling or geotagging.

For every asset capture: `asset_id, photographer, owner, signed_license_or_consent, capture_date, location, subject, allowed_uses, credit, alt_text, people_consent, publication_approval, source_original, focal_point, derivatives`. EXIF/GPS should be stripped from web derivatives unless explicitly authorized. Generate AVIF/WebP responsive derivatives; original archive stays in access-controlled storage. Search-result photographs are research references only, not approved site assets.

**Initial field target:** 2 outstanding Valencia hero photo candidates, 8–12 high-quality local culture/place photos, 12–20 support images and documented rights/consent; actual municipal seal only when authorized. Facebook leads are for identification and contact, not automatic web publication. Prefer authentic documentary photography over AI-generated 'real Valencia' scenes.

## 6. Editorial/publication roles
- **Project technical authority:** architecture, code quality, publication gate and source provenance enforcement.
- **Antigravity:** bounded implementation/visual testing executor on isolated branches, not the authority on municipal facts.
- **Municipal factual approver (TBD):** approves current service requirements, advisories, official terminology and public documents if official.
- **Editor (TBD):** drafts, captions, accessibility and scheduling; cannot self-approve sensitive notices.
- **Privacy/publication approver (TBD):** confirms release of contact information, photography and documents.

Mandatory workflow: source submitted -> claim and license checked -> content authored -> editorial review -> municipal approval when needed -> PR review -> publish -> dated review/expire/archive. Every news item needs a publication date; every service guide needs a next-review date.

## 7. Immediate discovery blockers
1. **Authority and audience:** is the project an officially commissioned LGU site or an independent public guide?
2. Actual current Citizen's Charter / office and hotline directory / local announcements source.
3. Legal permission to use municipal seal and publish documents.
4. Original photographs and permissions.
5. Domain and hosting ownership; no DNS or production changes during M00.
6. Assigned municipal reviewer and ongoing editorial steward.
