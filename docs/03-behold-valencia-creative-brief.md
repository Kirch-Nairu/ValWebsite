# V02 — BEHOLD VALENCIA: destination-led creative direction
Status: **working concept**, 2026-11-06. Not an approved LGU campaign, official slogan, trademark clearance, or publishing authorization.
Supersedes the civic-portal-first visual hierarchy in earlier planning. Public information remains essential, but **promotion, identity and visual impact lead**.

## 1. Product brief and approval audience
**Strategic product:** A flagship digital presence for **Valencia, Bohol** that tells a specific, emotionally resonant story about the municipality: heritage, nature, living culture, people, opportunity and municipal progress. It must communicate to the mayor, council, department heads, partners, residents, investors and visitors that Valencia deserves attention. The site should look exceptional in a two-minute executive presentation while being fully functional on ordinary phones.

**Decision-maker pitch:** “One town. One coherent digital identity. A high-end place-promotion experience that also gives the public reliable information.”

**Non-negotiable balance:** a civic website, not candidate advertising. Feature verified municipal achievements under neutral, attributed records; allow leadership portraits and welcome messaging when formally approved, but never fake endorsement, campaign promises, awards, metrics or current officeholders. If LGU authority is absent, present it as an independent creative proposal.

**Narrative pillars**
1. **Behold the Place** — living landscapes, coastline and inland geography, authentic morning/evening views, recognisable spaces.
2. **Behold Our Heritage** — Sto. Niño Church, local history, traditional architecture and documented meanings of place names.
3. **Behold Our Culture** — Sang-at Festival, rituals, local arts, cuisine, community celebrations.
4. **Behold Our People** — markets, fishers, farmers, youth, entrepreneurs, families, authentic portraits with consent.
5. **Behold Our Progress** — verifiable education, municipal infrastructure, livelihood, technology and partnerships.
6. **Behold Our Future** — credible investment and tourism potential, opportunities and a forward-looking editorial message grounded in approved sources.

## 2. Homepage: film-like opening, then story
1. **Immersive hero**: full-bleed *licensed* exceptional Valencia aerial/ground photograph; large expressive editorial type “BEHOLD VALENCIA.” with quiet “BOHOL, PHILIPPINES” context, subtle scroll cue, two clear actions “Explore Valencia” and “Discover Our Story”. A still image must be strong enough without video. Consider <15-second muted looping hero video only when authentic, licensed, captioned, adaptive and proven not to break mobile LCP/accessibility. No auto-audio.
2. **A short visual manifesto**: “A place shaped by people, heritage and possibilities” (draft, not factual municipal slogan). One clean paragraph, no hollow marketing superlatives.
3. **The Valencia edit**: large asymmetric photographs telling 3–4 stories (Heritage, Festival, Nature, Everyday Life); editorial image-caption patterns, not identical card grids.
4. **Sang-at / cultural calendar**: cultural feature, archive of dated editions and verified upcoming events; visually rich, never imply a past event is upcoming.
5. **Our People / Everyday Valencia**: human-centric documentary sequences from local creators, with consent and credits; livelihoods, places, markets, youth.
6. **Progress with substance**: 2–4 documented milestones with verified source/date (e.g. Anoyon High School classroom handover March 2026, 2026 Paleng-QR PH Plus coverage); neutral factual narratives, no uncited outcome metrics.
7. **Discover the municipality**: official sources with lightly styled facts/map, discovery links and travel context; no fake geolocation or hours.
8. **Stay connected**: current source-verified announcements/events, plus compact access to services and official records.
9. **Footer**: leadership/government/service/contact/transparent official status, approved media credits, dates, policies.

**Nav hierarchy** (maximum six, intentionally NOT service-first):
- Discover (`/discover`, places, nature and visitor info)
- Culture & Heritage (`/stories`, heritage, festivals, people)
- Our Valencia (`/valencia`, municipality profile/history/communities)
- Progress (`/progress`, projects and validated achievements)
- Updates (`/updates`, events/advisories)
- Government (`/government`, officeholders, departments, services, transparency, contact)
- Top right primary CTA: **Explore Valencia** (discovery entry) or **Plan Your Visit** only when verified logistics exist.
Navigation may be refined by user testing; maintain findability for services, notices and emergency information, even if one extra utility shortcut is required.

**Secondary routes:**
- `/visit` or `/discover/[slug]` — verified attraction and place guides with photos, safety/access notes.
- `/culture/sang-at` — documentary festival landing page, history, archive, image credits.
- `/people/[slug]` and `/stories/[slug]` — community profiles and long-form articles.
- `/progress/[slug]` — project narrative: date, scope, funder, sources, actual status (planned, active, complete); no impression of completed when not.
- `/government/services`, `/government/services/[slug]`, `/government/documents`, `/government/offices`, `/government/transparency`.
- `/contact` — approved information and route to municipal channels.

**Content tone:** Direct, locally rooted, polished, evocative but grounded; real names and specific places beat “hidden gem”, “world-class paradise”, empty investment promises and generated fake testimonials. Speak about people without treating them as decorative props.

## 3. Design experiments to run in Antigravity
**Direction A — Cinematic Place Editorial (default winner to beat):**
Strong hero photo, strong typographic anchors, rich dark/bright rhythm, editorial layouts, elegant hover and progressive image reveals. Local specificity must be visible immediately. Best for mayor/council demo.

**Direction B — Contemporary Heritage Museum:**
Warm material textures, archival timelining, thoughtful captions and curated photography, powerful storytelling. Useful for church and Sang-at preservation narratives.

**Direction C — Modern Municipal Destination Brand:**
Simplified, clear geometry, bold color, institutional legitimacy + tourism polish, adaptable to ongoing municipal communications. Useful for maintainability and government stakeholders.

Do not blend random effects. All candidates use exactly the same approved fact set and rights-cleared image assets or clearly identified gray-box photo placeholders. They must be compared fairly at 390 and 1440 widths.

**Type:** Oversized expressive editorial display face with accessible, restrained body sans; shortlist by screenshot and licenses. Do not duplicate an unrelated corporate brand identity automatically.
**Color:** Proposal from landscape and approved municipal identity: coastal dark green / marine blue / limestone warm white / muted sunlit gold. Final official seal usage and brand colors require LGU approval.
**Motion:** meaningful, sparse; 200–400ms micro-interactions; respect reduced motion, image loading and device capabilities. No mandatory WebGL, Three.js, heavy sliders, scroll jank or giant video autoplay.
**Asset budget:** set responsive image sizes, preload only true LCP hero, minimize blocking JS. Defend every JS island. A no-video fallback must retain the aesthetic.

## 4. Leadership and executive presentation
Prepare a five-screen approval sequence:
1. Immersive Valencia introduction.
2. Festival/heritage story with authentic photography and attribution.
3. People/places picture essay.
4. Municipal progress projects with precise source and verified status.
5. Quiet utility proof: access to announcements, documents and services on a mobile phone.

For leadership profiles: verify current function/acting status on the intended publication date; do not infer current status from 2025 election results alone. No flattering campaign-style claims, only approved institutional profiles and actual projects.

## 5. Editorial production structure
For each story: headline, one-sentence premise, 200–500-word sourced draft, authorized quotes/interviews, images and captions with rights, short mobile summary, date of event, publish/review date, place/barangay, source citations, editor, approver and status.
For each feature: cover image quality >= acceptable crop, distinctive place identity, verified location and story, meaningful action. No empty cards or made-up descriptions.
For each project: fiscal period, donor/partner, implementing body, published evidence, project status, dates and verified factual outcomes.

**Homepage acceptance**
- Within five seconds, a viewer should unmistakably know this is **Valencia, Bohol** and see a reason to keep scrolling.
- The two-minute demo should reveal at least three distinct local stories, no copied competitor template, no fake official branding.
- At 390px, photos remain powerful, type doesn't overlap, actions usable, nav understandable, and LCP acceptable.
- An important advisory and government service still reachable rapidly without making the homepage read like a helpdesk.
- Every hero/editorial photo is legitimately reusable, correctly credited and geolocated. No misleading unrelated Bohol scenery.

## 6. Brand and authority boundaries
“Behold Valencia” is the creative working line, inspired by the existing provincial destination language “Behold Bohol”, not proof of endorsement or mark clearance. Confirm municipal and provincial brand usage before release; keep a replaceable text lockup. Never use an altered official municipal seal as a novelty logo. Obtain municipal original vector art and guidance.
