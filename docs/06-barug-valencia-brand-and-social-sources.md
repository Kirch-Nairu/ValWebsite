Do **not** set `BEHOLD VALENCIA` as an alternative official tagline, alternate logo, page identity or headline without a separate creative decision.

The phrase must not be mechanically copied into every headline; storytelling should feel specific, confident and locally authentic.
- BARUG may also be described as a municipal governance acronym in news stories, with variants such as “Unified” vs “Unifying” in third-party sources; avoid asserting an official expansion until the LGU's own written identity source confirms the wording.
- Municipality/officeholder content should distinguish factual institutional stories from partisan promotion. Verified dates, role status and project attribution matter.
