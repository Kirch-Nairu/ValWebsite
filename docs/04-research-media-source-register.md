## 5. Acquisition/approval model
Fields for each asset:
`id, subject, barrio_barangay, candidate_origin, original_permalink, origin_type, captured_at, author, copyright_owner, rights_basis, license_evidence_path, model_release_if_needed, location_verified, attribution_text, intended_placement, orientation, resolution, crop_safety, approval_status, approver, approved_at, checksum, local_original_path, web_derivative_path`.

Workflow: discover -> source/original found -> geography verified -> photographer/contact/rights -> license/consent documented -> high-resolution original received -> accessibility caption/alt -> editorial approval -> optimize -> approved site media.
Status names: `candidate`, `contacting_owner`, `rights_cleared`, `approved`, `rejected`. Distinguish **research cache** (do not commit or publish) from publishable assets.

**Suggested photo requests:** 1–2 panoramic coastal/landscape hero sequences, 4 town-life/environment features, 4 heritage, 6 festival action/portraits, 6 livelihoods/community/culture, 4 municipal progress, 4 other landscapes/attractions. Video only from actual licensed original footage.
**Shot priority:** proof of town location + recognized architecture + Sang-at identities; ask municipal PIO/cultural office about official brand kit, festival photographer archive, seal vector and public-release permissions.

## 6. Contamination checks
- **Do NOT use cityofvalencia.gov.ph:** it is *Valencia City, Bukidnon*.
- **Do NOT use ValenciaNegOrTourismOffice:** it is *Valencia, Negros Oriental*.
- Some tourism aggregator pages falsely describe Valencia Bohol as an inland municipality without coastline, contrary to multiple official/place records. Never take aggregator prose as geographic truth.
- Old 2021 hall photos and mayor banners may depict an obsolete administration. Do not imply the image is current.
- Conflicts on founded year (1867 vs 1869) and Badian/Badiang naming require municipal confirmation.
